02 Things I've built

🚘 LiDAR–Camera 3D Tracking

YOLOv8 finds the cars, LiDAR points get projected into the image, and every car keeps its track ID. For the car ahead, LiDAR and camera time-to-collision are fused in a Kalman filter that shrugs off a broken LiDAR scan.

TTC error 0.94 s vs 5.20 s baseline · 35 keypoint pairs benchmarked

  • C++17
  • OpenCV
  • YOLOv8
  • KITTI

📡 LiDAR–Radar 3D Tracking

LiDAR knows where a car is, radar knows how fast it's closing. An IMM of unscented Kalman filters takes the best of both through cut-ins and hard braking on a simulated highway.

Fused position RMSE 0.06 m, velocity 0.27 m/s · 100 seeded runs

  • C++17
  • Eigen
  • IMM / UKF
  • Hungarian

📶 FMCW Radar Perception

From raw radar IQ samples all the way to tracked objects: range-Doppler FFTs, CFAR tuned by false-alarm rate, 4D MIMO angles, clustering and EKF tracks. It even fights off interference from other radars and multipath ghosts.

≈3.8 ms per frame · 100% detection, 0 false alarms · 1 cm range error

  • C++
  • GoogleTest
  • Benchmark
  • CI

🛰️ LiDAR 3D Detection & Tracking

No deep learning, just geometry: ground removal, range-image clustering, L-shape boxes, NDT ego-motion and Kalman tracks, with every choice measured against the alternatives. Wrapped as a ROS 2 node.

27 ms per frame for detection on one CPU core

  • C++17
  • PCL
  • Eigen
  • ROS 2

🧩 RangeNet Panoptic

RangeNet++ promised panoptic segmentation "coming soon" and was archived with the folder empty. I re-implemented the authors' own design, LPSAD, in PyTorch 2 so it trains on a 6 GB laptop GPU.

Every LiDAR point gets a class; every car and person gets an ID

  • PyTorch 2
  • SemanticKITTI
  • bf16

🤏 Panda: Classical vs Learned Grasping

A Franka Panda with a wrist camera picks up objects it has never seen. A geometric grasp detector goes head to head with a Faster R-CNN, judged by the lab's lift-rotate-shake protocol.

Grasps found on all 60 simulated placements · RelaxedIK-style IK in C++

  • ROS 2
  • MoveIt 2
  • PCL
  • PyTorch
