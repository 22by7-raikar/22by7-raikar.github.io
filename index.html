Selected work

LiDAR–Camera 3D Tracking

Vehicle detection with YOLOv8, LiDAR points projected into the image and clustered per detection, keypoint matching and persistent track IDs on KITTI. For the lead vehicle, LiDAR range and the camera's scale change are fused in a Kalman filter into a time-to-collision estimate that stays stable when either sensor misbehaves.

0.94 s
fused TTC error, vs 5.20 s for the nearest-point baseline
0.38 s
TTC jitter, lowest of all estimators
35
keypoint detector/descriptor pairs benchmarked

C++17 · OpenCV · ONNX Runtime · Kalman filtering · KITTI

LiDAR–Radar 3D Tracking

Multi-target vehicle tracking from LiDAR and radar with classical estimation throughout: an IMM of CTRV unscented Kalman filters, χ² gating, Hungarian assignment, log-likelihood-ratio track management and an L-shape box detector on ray-cast scans. A seeded highway simulator with a moving ego vehicle measures every design choice.

0.06 m
fused position RMSE (LiDAR only 0.08 m, radar only 0.33 m)
0.27 m/s
fused velocity RMSE
100
seeded runs per ablation

C++17 · Eigen · IMM / UKF · Hungarian assignment

FMCW Radar Perception

A real-time radar stack from raw IQ frames to tracked, classified objects: windowed range-Doppler FFT, CFAR with the threshold solved from a target false-alarm rate, TDM-MIMO 4D angle estimation, DBSCAN clustering and EKF tracking. Interference, clutter masking and multipath ghosts are simulated and mitigated.

3.8 ms
mean signal-chain latency per frame, one CPU core
100%
detection rate with zero false alarms on the default scene
1 cm
mean range error with a 1 m bin

C++ · GoogleTest · Google Benchmark · ASan/UBSan · CI

LiDAR 3D Detection & Tracking

Classical 3D object detection and multi-object tracking for spinning LiDAR: zone-based ground segmentation, range-image clustering, gravity-aligned L-shape boxes, NDT ego-motion and Kalman tracking with cascade Hungarian matching. Ground segmentation, clustering, box fitting and ego-motion are each compared against alternative methods with measurements, and a ROS 2 node wraps the pipeline.

27 ms
detection per frame on one 2.1 GHz core
73 ms
including NDT ego-motion
4
stages with measured comparisons of alternative methods

C++17 · PCL · Eigen · ROS 2

More projects

  • RangeNet Panoptic

    LiDAR panoptic segmentation on range images: a PyTorch 2 re-implementation of LPSAD on top of RangeNet++, which the original authors described but never released.

    Offset-based instance clustering · trains on a 6 GB laptop GPU

  • Panda Learned Grasping

    ROS 2 and MoveIt 2 pick-and-place comparing a classical top-surface grasp detector with a multi-grasp Faster R-CNN, under a YCB benchmarking protocol.

    Grasps found on all 60 simulated placements · RelaxedIK-style IK in C++

  • VLA Guidance on SO-101

    Training-free, depth-based cost guidance inside a SmolVLA flow-matching sampler to avoid obstacles unseen in training, with a CBF-QP safety filter as backup.

    In progress

  • PlanTF Extension

    Extensions to a learned motion planner for nuPlan: geometric mode re-ranking, an 84-scenario benchmark and a TensorRT C++ runtime.

    NR-CLS 0.8401 → 0.8478 · 1.2–1.7 ms inference vs ~20 ms

  • edge_audio

    A C++ runtime that decides whether audio is worth transcribing before spending Whisper compute on it, plus a streaming voice-command loop.

    F1 0.920 vs 0.807 rule gate · 33% fewer ASR calls · ~1 µs per decision

  • On-Device Voice Interface

    A fully on-device speech interface for a robot: microphone-array front end, Whisper, validated LLM tool calls and barge-in.

    In progress

  • Marine PTZ Tracker

    A pan/tilt camera rig that detects and follows a small marine target, with a microcontroller enforcing actuator limits.

    p95 capture-to-command ~58 ms · YOLO11n + BoT-SORT

  • FMCW Radar Target Detection

    77 GHz waveform design, range-Doppler processing and 2D CA-CFAR detection.

    MATLAB and NumPy implementations

  • Learned Dynamics + MPC

    Neural-network vehicle dynamics identified from simulation, used by a model-predictive controller to track an 835 m path.

    ROS · Gazebo · PyTorch · CVXPY
